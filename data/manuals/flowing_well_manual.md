# Natural Flowing Well Operating Procedures & Guidelines
**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-FL-004  
**Category:** Standard Operating Manual  
**Applies To:** Naturally Flowing Wells (No Artificial Lift)  
**Revision:** 1.8  
**Author:** Production Engineering  

---

## 1. Scope
Applies to high-pressure discovery wells producing to flowlines without downhole or surface mechanical pumping assistance.

## 2. Thermal Production Verification Rule
- **Threshold Limit:** **18.0°F**.
- **Rule:** $\Delta T = T_{wellhead} - T_{ambient}$.
  - If $\Delta T \ge 18.0^\circ\text{F}$: Active high-velocity gas and oil flow from reservoir depth. Classified as **PRODUCING**.
  - If $\Delta T < 18.0^\circ\text{F}$: Well has loaded up with liquids or sand bridging has occurred. Classified as **NOT PRODUCING**.
  - **Exception - Planned Shut-in:** Choke valve calibration or gathering line pigging maintenance.

## 3. Downtime Accounting
- Non-producing inspection = 7 days downtime.
- Two consecutive non-producing inspections = 14 days downtime.
- Missed inspection = 0 days downtime.
