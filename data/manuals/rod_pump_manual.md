# Rod Pump (Beam Pumping Unit) Operating Manual
**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-RP-003  
**Category:** Standard Operating Manual  
**Applies To:** Rod Pump (Beam Pumping Unit) Wells  
**Revision:** 2.5  
**Author:** Artificial Lift Operations  

---

## 1. Scope
Applies to sucker rod pumping systems, conventional beam units, and hydraulic pumping jacks.

## 2. Thermal Production Verification Rule
- **Threshold Limit:** **12.0°F**.
- **Rule:** $\Delta T = T_{wellhead} - T_{ambient}$.
  - If $\Delta T \ge 12.0^\circ\text{F}$: Well fluid is reaching surface under reciprocating pump action. Classified as **PRODUCING**.
  - If $\Delta T < 12.0^\circ\text{F}$: Sucker rod parted, traveling valve stuck, or unit stopped. Classified as **NOT PRODUCING**.
  - **Exception - Planned Shut-in:** Surface engine maintenance or gear box oil changes explicitly logged.

## 3. Downtime Accounting
- Each weekly non-producing inspection = 7 days downtime.
- Consecutive non-producing inspections = 14 days downtime.
- Missed inspection = 0 days downtime.
